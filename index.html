ABOUT ME

Turning moments
into stories that last.

I'm Mia Alves, a video editor and content producer with a strong focus on YouTube content and digital storytelling. I've worked closely with creators and events like anime conventions, helping bring engaging stories to life through video.

I'm passionate about how social media shapes the way we connect, consume, and understand the world - and how that influence will continue to evolve. My work is driven by storytelling, with an emphasis on rhythm, emotion, and audience engagement. I believe the videos we create today become part of our story - a digital footprint of who we were and what we chose to share with the world.
